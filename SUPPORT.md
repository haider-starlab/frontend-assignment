# Questions?

Ambiguity in the brief is not a trap. If something is unclear, ask — we'd rather
answer than have you lose two hours guessing.

**Email:** haider@cloudnebula.in
**Response time:** within 4 hours during business hours (09:00–18:00 IST,
Mon–Fri). Outside that, next working morning.

## Good things to ask about

- A requirement that seems to contradict another one
- Whether a particular scope cut is acceptable
- Anything that looks like a bug in `src/mock/`
- Environment or setup trouble — don't lose your first evening to a Node version

## Things we won't answer

- Which library to choose (that's part of what we're assessing)
- How to implement something (likewise)
- Reviews of partial work before submission

## If you get properly stuck

Make a reasonable assumption, write it down in `DECISIONS.md`, and keep moving.
Documented assumptions score points. A blocked afternoon scores nothing.

## Extensions

If something has come up — illness, work crunch, family — just tell us. We'll
move the deadline. We're trying to see how you build software, not how you
handle an artificial deadline.
